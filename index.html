Telluris Labs develops software and solutions for geospatial data analysis, management, and automation.

Our solutions range from focused tools to end-to-end data processing and analysis, using an approach that emphasizes scientific rigor, reproducible methods, and reliable results. Comprehensive documentation and testing are integral to our development process.

As part of our commitment to open science, we develop and maintain open-source Python packages, with a focus on scientific research and environmental analysis applications. Current packages include atmoflux for atmospheric and surface energy-flux calculations and thermadex (in development) for thermal-comfort and heat-stress analysis.
